ATTRIBUTION NOTICE:

This enhanced version builds upon the original HTML Violations Analyzer 
developed by Florian Hantke as part of research published in:

"HTML Violations and Where to Find Them: A Longitudinal Analysis of 
Specification Violations in HTML" - IMC 2022

Original repository: https://github.com/cispa/html-violations-analyzer
Research paper: https://swag.cispa.saarland/papers/hantke2022violations.pdf

The enhancements include advanced analytics, visualization capabilities, 
local file processing, and expanded documentation while maintaining the 
core architecture and methodology of the original work.
