The 4th eMerging eLearning Conference is on from November 19 to 21, 2005, in Abu Dhabi. World class experts in eLearning will be speaking at the event providing an excellent forum in which to collaborate and debate the latest trends in eLearning. This year’s theme of Technology Enhanced Learning includes mobile learning (introduced by Ellen Wagner) and learning process innovation (introduced by Jay Cross) plus many more.
