Passports and return documents issuance

UAE MOFAIC provides the service of issuing an emergency passport

Issue Special Passport

The Special Passport is an official travel document issued to a certain group of Emirati citizens to facilitate their performance of a variety of significant tasks serving the UAE interests. The Passport grants holders some privileges including ease of procedures and transactions as well as movement and travel. Service Procedures 1- Login to MoFAIC’s website by entering the username and password shared with entities authorized to apply for the service 2- Select one of the Special Passport service options. Click on “Start Service” to fill in the service application form, and attach required documents. 3- You will receive an Email with a Reference Number confirming successful application. 4- You will receive an Email informing you that the Passport is ready for collection.

Issue Mission Passport

Request to Issue Mission Passport. A Mission Passport is an official travel document issued to a special group of government employees (UAE nationals and foreign nationals) to help them perform a variety of significant tasks serving UAE interests. The Passport grants holders some privileges including ease of procedures, transactions, circulation and travel. Service Procedures &nbsp;&nbsp;- UAE Nationals: 1- Login to MoFAIC’s website by entering the username and password shared with entities authorized to apply for the service. 2- Select one of the Mission Passport service options. Click on “Start Service” to fill in the service application form, and attach required documents. 3- You will receive an Email with a Reference Number confirming successful application. 4- You will receive an Email informing you that the Passport is ready for collection. &nbsp;&nbsp;- Foreign Nationals: 1- Login to MoFAIC’s website by entering the username and password shared with entities authorized to apply for the service. 2- Select one of the Mission Passport service options. Click on “Start Service” to fill in the service application form, and attach required documents. 3- You will receive an Email with a Reference Number confirming successful application. 4- You will receive an Email informing you that the letter to Federal Authority for Identity and Citizenship is ready for collection.
