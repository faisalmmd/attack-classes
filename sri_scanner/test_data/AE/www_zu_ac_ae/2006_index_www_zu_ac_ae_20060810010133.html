ZU Interns at UAE London Embassy
Two International Studies students from Abu Dhabi, Nouf Hamad al Hamiri and Fatima Muhammad al Mehairi, are learning about diplomacy and the workings of an Emirati embassy in London. The embassy in London is the largest UAE embassy and the students have learned how several different sections function.

Zayed University moves to new Dubai Campus
After 8 years at Al-Twar, Zayed University moves to a new purpose-built campus in Al Ruwayyah in August.

The new ZU campus is part of Dubai's Academic City complex and is located near the Al-Ain Road and Emirates Road. Prospective visitors to the new campus are invited to study the maps available online.
More information on the move to the new campus is available at www.zu.ac.ae/move. Questions regarding the move can be sent to move@zu.ac.ae.
