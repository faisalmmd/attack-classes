Getting into gear for the new academic year
The new academic year began this week on both campuses of the University. Classes began on August 27. The previous week saw hundreds of new students attending orientation sessions.

Zayed University will observe the Al Isra Wal Meraj Holiday on Wednesday, August 31. Classes and administrative functions will resume on Saturday, September 3.

Sheikh Nahayan addresses annual convocation
His Highness Sheikh Nahayan Bin Mabarak Al Nahayan, Minister of Education and President of Zayed University, gave the keynote address at the University's annual convocation which was held on August 21 at the Emirates Palace Hotel in Abu Dhabi. Faculty and staff from both campuses heard Sheikh Nahayan pledge the university community to nurture and sustain the values that have guided its progress during the past seven years. Sheikh Nahayan noted that the the University faces the challenge of maintaining a high level of achievement in the rapidly changing global environment. He regarded the challenge as a significant opportunity for further growth and development.

Zayed University Convocation
At ZU's 8th annual convocation, University vice-president Dr Hanif Al Qassimi stressed the University's academic, cultural and economic connections. He noted that ZU's future as a premier university is about the connections it makes as it interacts with its students and reaches out to national and international communities. Dr. Hanif went on to thank members of the University community for their commitment and hard work.
