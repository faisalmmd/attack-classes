ZU students visit Sheikh Mohammed bin Zayed Al Nahyan
A Zayed University student delegation, led by Zayed University Vice-President Dr. Hanif Hassan, has visited General Sheikh Mohammed bin Zayed Al Nahyan, Abu Dhabi Crown Prince and Deputy Supreme Commander of the UAE Armed Forces. The students, who had visited the UN Headquarters in Geneva, Switzerland earlier this year, gave a presentation on their trip.

General Sheikh Mohammed praised the excellent standards of education attained by Zayed University students. He said "UAE female nationals have fulfilled the expectations and ambitions of their country and are poised to take a leading role in helping the UAE to progress in the coming years."

Workshop series for Ministry of Education Supervisors
Zayed University's Center for Professional Development of UAE Educators has been conducting a series of workshops with Ministry of Education Supervisors. These Supervisors are involved with an action research project under the guidance of Drs Barbara Harold and Lauren Stevenson, and the Director of the Center, Dr Kenneth Carr. This major, intensive project runs for three months, and is facilitated by a series of workshops, presentations and planning sessions. The project is investigating issues that are important for the professional development of teachers in the UAE. Questionnaires and interviews have been administered in the different curriculum subject areas, and the data collected is currently being analyzed. Based on the findings, the supervisors will then begin the 'action' phase on the project, and help teachers develop their skills as they contribute to better student learning and achievement in schools. The project is one of many that the Ministry of Education has contracted the Center and the College of Education, to undertake. Fifty subject Supervisors from the curriculum areas of Arabic and Islamic Studies, Mathematics, English, Social Sciences, and Science are presently involved with this three-month project.

ZU to develop entrepreneurship research project with Mohammad bin Rashid Establishment for Young Business Leaders
Zayed University has signed a memorandum of understanding with Mohammad bin Rashid Establishment for Young Business Leaders to work together to conduct research into entrepreneurship in the United Arab Emirates. The organizations have agreed to cooperate, collaborate and support each other to complete the Global Entrepreneurship Monitor (GEM) in the UAE to the standards and criteria of the Global Entrepreneurship Research Association. The memorandum was signed by Dr. Hanif Hassan, Vice-President, Zayed University and Abdul Baset Al Janahi, CEO of Mohammed bin Rashid Establishment for Young Business Leaders.
