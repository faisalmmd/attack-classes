Publication of a Peer-Reviewed Academic Research Book

Dr. Yoosuf Cader, Associate Professor (CBS) edited his second book on knowledge management, "Knowledge Management Integrated- Concepts and Practice" (2007), published by Heidelberg Press (Australia). This research book has chapter contributions from seventeen international authors with five chapters from authors based in the Middle East, three of whom are from Zayed University.

Ghleelah Project - Redesigning a Community

A group of five Zayed University students has put forward plans to redesign a village in Ras Al Khaimah to meet the needs of twenty-first century living. The students have developed a new ethical and sustainable model for the community of Ghleelah, whose younger residents are leaving their birthplace for better opportunities in major urban areas. After meeting villagers and surveying the area, the students put together a blueprint for regeneration, which combines traditional and modern architecture and building materials, implements alternative cooling techniques and limits the ecological impact on the community.

Zayed University Sets Up Literacy Conference

Zayed University will organize the First Information Literacy Network Professional Development Conference on December 8 and 9. The conference will build new social technology skills and learn about information literacy in the region. The meeting will gather information literacy professionals, librarians and academic experts in the GCC. The event will be held under the theme "Social Technologies and Information Literacy" and participants will have the opportunity to share ideas and exchange expertise.

Faculty Receive a Prestigious Award for their Research Paper

Dr. Jay Squalli and Dr Kenneth Wilson from ZU's Economic & Policy Research Unit (EPRU) have recently earned a prestigious award for their research paper titled "A new measure of trade openness". Dr Squalli has just returned from presenting the paper at the Northeast Business & Economics 2007 Annual Conference, held on November 7th and 8th in Connecticut, USA where the paper was acclaimed with the 'Best Paper Award'. More than 100 papers were presented at this refereed conference and the paper faced stiff competition from several leading US scholars.

Ten Years of Excellence

Zayed University is commemorating the beginning of its tenth year of operations by welcoming His Highness Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid Al Maktoum, Vice President, Prime Minister and Ruler of Dubai, to the Dubai campus the morning of November 5. There, he will officially open the new campus and inaugurate the celebration of the University's tenth anniversary.

Throughout the 2007-2008 academic year, ZU will be holding special events on both the Abu Dhabi and Dubai campuses for faculty, staff, students and alumni to celebrate ten years of success.
