The UAE.. A Promising Global Investment Hub

The UAE boasts first-class investment environment and integrated logistical infrastructure. In 2020, it launched the UAE 50 Economic Plan in line with the UAE Centennial 2071 goals to boost national economy, increase the GDP, develop the local business environment, strengthen the position of UAE companies globally, and provide investors with access to markets across the world.
