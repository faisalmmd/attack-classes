Welcome Statement


It's a great opportunity for us to welcome your visit to the UAE Central Bank Website. Work is still in progress. Nevertheless, we hope you can already find some of the information you are looking for.

There are many aspects to the role the Central Bank plays in supporting the national economy of the United Arab Emirates. In addition to acting as the bank to the other banks operating in the country, it is also the banker and financial advisor to the Government. It is the responsibility of the Central Bank to formulate and implement the country's Banking, Credit and Monetary Policy in order to realize and achieve the Economic Policy objectives, including price stability, and to support the U.A.E. dirham, guaranteeing its value, stability and its free convertibility into all currencies.

You may want to start with "About Us", which details the objectives, monetary instruments, organization, as well as the Board of Directors of our institution and our branches all over the country, in addition to "Contact Us" facility.

"Laws and Regulations" includes the Central Bank law of 1980, which is the Founding Act, defining the objectives, main instruments for monetary policy, the organizational structure, as well as the task entrusted to the Board of Directors. This section displays also other laws and circulars.

"Publications" contains our reports and bulletins for the last three years. It also gives links to relevant work on the UAE conducted by international institutions, particularly the International Monetary Fund and Financial Action Task Force (FATF).

"Events" contains the announcement for coming events, mainly conferences and workshops we are organizing, to increase awareness about Central Bank activities and to ensure the soundness of our financial system. This section also contains an archive of last events.

The "News Room" was updated to publish press releases, public statements as well as the Governor's speeches and interviews.

A special section "Banks and other financial institutions" lists all local and foreign banks operating in our country, as well as other financial institutions. It also lists the services offered by these institutions and their prices, a step toward more disclosure and transparency.

A special section is devoted to "Currency", particularly, commemorative Notes and Coins, which are displayed for sale.

Last but not least, we invite you to use "Feedback", a facility that allows you to get in touch with us and to enquire about our activities.



Sultan Bin Naser Al - Suwaidi
