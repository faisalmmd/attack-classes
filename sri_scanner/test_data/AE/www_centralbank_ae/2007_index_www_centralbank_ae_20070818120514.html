Welcome

Welcome to the Web site of the Central Bank of the UAE . You will find information, data and analysis on Central Bank activity, as well as on the UAE financial sector and economy.

The Central Bank plays a vital role in the national economy. Its main responsibility is to formulate and implement the banking, credit and monetary policy to ensure price stability, and to support the Dirham and its free convertibility; in addition to acting as "the bank of banks" and the banker and financial advisor to the Government.

About Us, details the objectives of the monetary policy, monetary instruments, organization of the Central Bank, as well as its Board of Directors.

Laws and Regulations includes the Union Law No. 10 of 1980 as well as other related laws and regulations.

Publications contains reports and bulletins for the last three years. It also links the recent diligence conducted on the UAE by international institutions, particularly the International Monetary Fund (IMF) and Financial Action Task Force (FATF).

Currency, features the various currency denominations in circulation

Events showcases the conferences, seminars, workshops and other initiatives of the Central Bank.

News Room publishes press releases, public statements as well as the Governor's speeches and interviews.

Banks and other financial institutions lists the services offered, and their prices, a step toward more disclosure and transparency.

Commemorative Coins features the various commemorative notes and coins, which are displayed for sale.

Feedback is a facility that allows you to get in touch with us and to enquire about our activities.
