Academic

We offer two academically accredited programmes that aim to inform and educate students with indispensable knowledge and skills to succeed in their diplomatic careers. We work with Internationally recognised faculty members, a growing library and staff.

how to get started

Executive Training

We provide tailored and practical training programmes for members of the UAE diplomatic corps and employees of relevant government entities. Designed and implemented by us, the curriculum is taught by AGDA’s in-house faculty and external trainers.

view upcoming courses








Research

We contribute to domestic and international debates on issues relevant to the UAE’s foreign policy and diplomacy, and act as an impactful source of analysis for the UAE government as well as help build student diplomats’ research and analytical capabilities.
